# Website Idea
Interactive site with clear direction thanks to a neat onboarding page that shows users how to use it and what it's for. ***See: lemonade.com***

## What it is
- Narrative
    - Interactive narrative that unfolds as you move through the opening screens. At first, you just fill out boring stuff. *What is your name?*, *What is today's date*, *What time is it?*. Each of these questions is the only thing on the screen, on a black background, and there's just one text box below them. They appear one at a time, and there's a slick fade out/fade in animation for each.
    - After "what time is it", it goes dark, at the top left, there are some lines of code forming and being written one character at a time, very fast, to give the appearance of the website doing some sort of mysterious work.
    - More text starts appearing, one line at a time:
        - "50 years ago, there was a contest..."
        - "One hundred competitors... and only one came out on top"
        - "His name was..."
    - After that last line, the screen explodes into colorful paint-splatter-like, particle based beauty, and in the center smashes down the name "Disco Dan", and a start button fades in.

## Games
- There will now be a series of games that you have to play to get through the website.
    - The name animals until failure game. Basically, it there will be white text at the top that says "Name animals until failure" and a text box below. The user enters the names of animals, and for each one that's in the list, they get a point. When they get one right, the text box shoots a white particle out to the left and right (just one on each side) and a little score counter under the title text updates. The word that they wrote goes under the text box. Any incorrect animals (words they enter that aren't animals), the letters of their word turn to dust and fade away, and the text box shakes for a second before stopping. Here's the catch: the word list changes. For the first five words, use the animal list. As long as the user has words on that list, they work. But after five correct animals, just tell the user that they're wrong, that's not an animal. Display funny, serious, deadpan, sarcastic messages each time they get one wrong after that point. After five messages, a hint will appear above the title, that says "did you try disco dan?" if the user then inputs "disco dan" or "Disco Dan" or any combo of upper and lowercase, they win the game and it all fades out and a "next" button appears in the center of the screen.
    - The wordle game. It's literally just wordle, but the answer is one of the words that the player inputted in at the start of the website. So those words must contain 5 letters. Edit the start so that the line under the text turns red and under that line a message says "Entries must contain five characters", if they don't have five characters.
