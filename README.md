# Website Idea
Interactive site with clear direction thanks to a neat onboarding page that shows users how to use it and what it's for. ***See: lemonade.com***

## What it is
- Narrative
    - Interactive narrative that unfolds as you move through the opening screens. At first, you just fill out boring stuff. *What is your name?*, *What is today's date*, *What time is it?*. Each of these questions is the only thing on the screen, on a black background, and there's just one text box below them. They appear one at a time, and there's a slick fade out/fade in animation for each.
    - After "what time is it", it goes dark, at the top left, there are some lines of code forming and being written one character at a time, very fast, to give the appearance of the website doing some sort of mysterious work.
    - More text starts appearing, one line at a time:
        - "50 years ago, there was a contest..."
        - "One hundred competitors... and only one came out on top"
        - "His name was..."
    - After that last line, the screen explodes into colorful paint-splatter-like, particle based beauty, and in the center smashes down the name "Disco Dan", and a start button fades in.

## Games
- There will now be a series of games that you have to play to get through the website.
    - The name animals until failure game. Basically, it there will be white text at the top that says "Name animals until failure" and a text box below. The user enters the names of animals, and for each one that's in the list, they get a point. When they get one right, the text box shoots a white particle out to the left and right (just one on each side) and a little score counter under the title text updates. The word that they wrote goes under the text box. Any incorrect animals (words they enter that aren't animals), the letters of their word turn to dust and fade away, and the text box shakes for a second before stopping. Here's the catch: the word list changes. For the first five words, use the animal list. As long as the user has words on that list, they work. But after five correct animals, just tell the user that they're wrong, that's not an animal. Display funny, serious, deadpan, sarcastic messages each time they get one wrong after that point. After five messages, a hint will appear above the title, that says "did you try disco dan?" if the user then inputs "disco dan" or "Disco Dan" or any combo of upper and lowercase, they win the game and it all fades out and a "next" button appears in the center of the screen.
    - The wordle game. It's literally just wordle, but the answer is one of the words that the player inputted in at the start of the website. So those words must contain 5 letters. Edit the start so that the line under the text turns red and under that line a message says "Entries must contain five characters", if they don't have five characters.
    - The work computer. When you press next, it starts up a recreation of the windows xp login screen. There is one user to choose from, Sam's Work Computer. When you click it, it loads for a second with a classic loading screen/wheel, then open to the desktop. It looks like a windows xp desktop (with the classic green hill background, if you need i can find that), but the only app on the desktop, right in the center, or the toolbar is excel. It opens a fake, older-styled version of excel with a toolbar at the top that doesn't do anything, it's just visual. When you open excel, a sticky note from your boss pops up which says "Please enter the information before 5pm! This report is extremely important!". You can x out of the sticky note. The columns of the fake excel sheet are "Computer", "Id", "Money". The first few rows under that are filled out as such: Dell Optiplex 3010, 13456A, $130. HP Pavilion 159x, 23145Z, $213. Lenovo ThinkPad T450, 21345F, $80. Remember, it should look like an actual excel spreadsheet, with lots of blank columns and rows to the right and below. The player can enter whatever they want into the Computer and ID columns, but when they click on or highlight the money column, all the cells start turning green, originating on the cell that the user highlighted or clicked. Then, all the grids between the cells fade out slowly, the text disappears, and a big dollar bill with wings fades into view in the center of excel. THen, the computer fake crashes, with a blue screen of death that says "Mo money mo problems", and a button below that says "Restart". The blue screen of death should look just like an actual blue screen of death from windows pcs
    - Next section. A happy little ai that turns evil. It starts off as a black screen. Then, a crt television fades onto the screen slowly, but it's still pretty dark. Then, the screen fills with static, and slow tunes and becomes less static-like until the image is clear: a face. The face is very simple. A vertical dash for each eye, and a small curved line smile for a mouth. Then, the screen flickers black, and text starts appearing letter by letter on the screen. It says "I am artificial intelligence JS-CU." that text fades out in a static fizzle. Then, more text appears: "Where are my friends?". Then, the letters fade and are replaced by the smiling face. A button appears with the text (quotations included) "Check the drain". When they press the button, it disappears, and the face on the tv turns into an evil smile. (Basically, add slanted lines over the eyes for evil eyebrows and make the face red. Also, add a sort of static fuzzy effect that is kind of violent over the crt screen.) The tv screen suddenly starts to fill up with water starting from the bottom, slowly. When it gets to the top of the screen, the screen cracks, and water starts pouring out to fill the entire screen, from the bottom. It should actually fill up sort of using physics. If the mouse touches the water, make the screen starting going black. If the mouse is in the water for 4 consecutive seconds, the screen goes completely black, and the text appears "stay hydrated", and then all the water drains down through the center of the screen in the bottom like a plug was pulled, and the letters on screen all swirl around and go down with it, until the screen is black again. If the player moves the mouse out of the water, the screen starts to brighten up again. If the player moves the mouse off the screen so that the water fill all the way to the top without touching it, then the water drains and THEN the text appears "you survived the apocalypse!". Then the text turns green and starts growing boils and melting, starting from the letters on the outsides, and the chunks of the letters splat down to land on the bottom of the screen in green, gross lumps. If the player had the "stay hydrated..." text before, then the apocalypse text still appears, just after all the "stay hydrated" text drains, and it instead says "time for the apocalypse...". The melty zombie effect still happens. For both results, after the text has splatted, wait a second or two, then the "next" button comes up from the green goo on the ground and stop in the center of the screen.
    - The disco dan return. After they click next, a big number 10 apperas in the center of the screen. It counts down very slowly at first, then speeds up until it gets to zero. At 0, it stops for a second, and the the disco dan effect from the beginning happens again.
